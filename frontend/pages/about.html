About Bazaar-Connect

Who We Are

Bazaar-Connect is a locally inspired B2B surplus exchange platform built for Malindi’s hospitality ecosystem. We connect hotels, resorts, and event venues with charities, farmers, and recycling partners to ensure surplus food and supplies are redistributed—not wasted.

Born from the realities of a tourism-driven town, Bazaar-Connect focuses on solving problems that large national platforms often overlook: bulk event waste, time-sensitive surplus, and limited access to smartphones.

Why We Exist

Every day in Malindi, perfectly usable food is discarded after weddings, conferences, and hotel buffets—while nearby communities face food shortages and farmers struggle with rising input costs.

We exist to change that.

Bazaar-Connect transforms surplus into opportunity by using real-time technology, offline access, and circular economy thinking to create value for everyone involved.

Our Mission

To reduce food and resource waste in Malindi by enabling fast, fair, and inclusive redistribution through technology built for local needs.

Our Vision

  • No quality surplus goes to waste
  • Hotels operate responsibly and efficiently
  • Local farmers and charities benefit directly
  • Technology works for smartphones and basic phones

Features

Real-Time Surplus Listings

Hotels and event organizers can list surplus food or supplies instantly, with automatic expiry timers to ensure items are claimed while still usable.

Instant Notifications

Using real-time messaging technology, receivers get immediate alerts when surplus becomes available nearby—reducing delays and spoilage.

Dual User System

Bazaar-Connect is built with two dedicated user roles:

  • Donors: Hotels, resorts, conference venues
  • Receivers: Charities, farmers, composters, small vendors

Offline-First USSD Access

  • Receive surplus alerts
  • View available items
  • Claim surplus using basic phones

Hotel-to-Farm Circular Exchange

  • Food scraps → compost
  • Compost → fertilizer
  • Fertilizer → fresh produce for hotels

Secure & Time-Sensitive Database

Every listing is tracked with timestamps, user verification, and status updates to maintain accountability and transparency.

Impact

Economic Impact

Environmental Impact

Measuring Success
