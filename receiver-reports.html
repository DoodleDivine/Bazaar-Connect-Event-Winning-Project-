This page reports guiding information on the site.

If you are a new user, please read the information below

To return, click the return button on the webpage.

Donor Guide

As a donor, your dashboard has 'Total Products' and 'Total Orders'.

You can only see the products you've added on the product page

and see the businesses that have ordered you're products on the order page.

There is also a given expiry date when you add a product for reliancy.

Receiver Guide

As a receiver, your dashboard shows the available products at

the current time. (this is the same for the receivers product page)

If there are no products at all, then the product page is empty

at the moment.

You can place orders on whatever product you'd like to have.

Safety Guide

Our database is not strong at the moment,

but it gets the job done.

If you get scammed, contact us through our

contact site and provide the scammers

information using the order page.

However, we advise you not to actually place orders

on this website as it is a prototype concept.

As time passes on, we will expand security and customer safety

so that all users will have a great experience.
